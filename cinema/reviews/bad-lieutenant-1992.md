I really enjoyed this.  I didn't find it as bleak as I was led to believe it might be from the reviews I'd seen.  It's not exactly uplifting of course.  

Keitel's performance is really brilliant and given the film is completely centered around his character it's just as well!

I thought the ending was great.  I was a bit like "Eh? Why'd he do that?", then in the very last scene it became clear...

It's a great looking 4k disc too!

As an aside, I enjoy the later Werner Herzog/Nic Cage Bad Lieutenant as well.
