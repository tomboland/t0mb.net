Second watch. An absolute pleasure to behold from start to end.

This is an incredible looking film. The angles, framing, composition, lighting, contrast, camera movement; everything is just so great looking. More than that though, it's the storytelling that's brilliant, and that's the magic. It's all very well having every frame be a painting, but this isn't a painting, it's a living breathing story and everything is working together in perfect unison to tell that story.
