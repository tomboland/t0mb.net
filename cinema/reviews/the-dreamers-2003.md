This film is absolutely full of beautiful scenes.  I love that its transgressiveness upsets people too.  I greatly enjoyed all the film references; well I say all but I will have missed many of them I'm sure, so I have a reason to watch it again in future!

The ending didn't land for me at all, in particular the very last moments with the gendarmerie running at the protestors with the slow frame-rate;  it looked wholly unconvincing!  95% of the film is absolutely exquisite.  I do wonder if the abrupt ending was a nod to Godard too; but it still didn't land for me!  I get that it kind of snaps them out of this insulated little world they've been in, but still; I didn't like the ending at all.  The film didn't do much to lay down the context there either.
