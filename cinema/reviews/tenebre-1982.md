The more I explore cinema, the more I enjoy quite idiosyncratic stuff that I might have scoffed at once upon a time.  I'm talking about far fetched plots, melodramatic acting, and best of all, things being implied instead of being shown in all their glory.  This film is full of that stuff, and it only added to my enjoyment.  There's some absolutely great soundtrack stuff going on too, and I really loved the visual style.  My first Dario Argento film, and I think I'll dig into some more soon!
