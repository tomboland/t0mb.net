On paper I really thought I'd like this. The ritualistic vibe and symbolism and whatnot; I just found it tedious though.  I thought about switching off about an hour into it and the next hour and twenty was just grueling really.  I didn't think much of the camerawork or visuals; half the time it seemed like the kind of pointless camera movement you see from AI generated videos; and half of the time half of what you were seeing was computer generated and obviously so!  This is the third Eggers film I haven't really liked.  So far it's just The Lighthouse which I do like; and I really like that a _lot_!
