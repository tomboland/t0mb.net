Watched 4K80, and I have to say, it's nothing like the experience of 4K77, and I gather that's simply that the source material was so poor in comparison.  I'm currently download a "grindhouse" version of a 70mm print, with some basic colour grading which I'm looking forward to seeing, and I'm also led to believe that there are several 35mm sources being worked on as we speak that will result in a v2.0 of 4K80.  It's still cool to see.  I won't review the film too much itself.  It's a great film.  What I will say is that this is like many other sequels where on first impression it might seem Hollywood saw a successful film and just decided to throw money at it to make something more full of action and chases, a bombastic score and just generally more extra in every way, and while this film does have all that, it seems  not to suffer from it.  I think it has a very different history explaining part of that (it wasn't just Hollywood throwing money at making something formulaic in order to multiply it at the box office), and the dark and "morally unsatisfying" ending (by Hollywood standards) is a breath of fresh air too.  It's an incredible film.  I hope to see a good version of it one day; it might even be that Disney produce a good version, if rumours are to be believed!
