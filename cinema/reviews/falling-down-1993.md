I think it's been 25 years or more since I've seen this, and my 4k arrived today, replacing the blu ray I bought last year and which I didn't get around to watching yet. 

I'm going to basically start and end by saying the film, and the 4k release, are essentially perfect in every respect.

As an addendum, I despair seeing people trying to figure out how this film and the 'tagonist fit into their moral framework; consequently, I think I enjoy the film more than someone preoccupied with moral mental gymnastics.  

There is not a single thing I would change about this film; not one thing!
