I left the cinema with a sense of dissatisfaction. I think the subject matter itself could be interesting, but to spend the entire film exploring it only at a very superficial level is frustrating. It seems I'm going to need to make my own food for thought here because the film doesn't give you much to chew on.  I didn't feel any sort of genuine quandary.  It's all "poor little parents; sad face; nasty authorities; angry face".  

It avoids being completely partisan, but it also avoids asking any really difficult questions, and it completely avoids questioning itself throughout.  

The bleak landscape and settings are nice and everything.  The bleak atmosphere, the sad faces, that's really all I got from this film.  The camera is very passive.  You are pretty much just observing proceedings; if only the proceedings were actually interesting...

I will say I appreciate the film eschewing the most egregious of manipulative tropes such as tear-jerking music and the like. 

In conclusion, the film acts like a serious, sombre inquiry into what could be a very challenging subject.  It's heavy on the bleak, sad faces, and light on the substantial exploration of the subject.  No food for thought; very dissatisfying.
