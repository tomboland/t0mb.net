I bought the blu ray a while ago on eBay.  I didn't know anything about it really, and in the time since it arrived I completely forgot pretty much anything else I knew about it in the first place.  Anyway it was looking at me from my film shelf so I decided to give it a whirl without overthinking it, you dig?  

I got about 20 minutes in and it was only just starting to occur to me that it wasn't actually from the 70s! Did they use a time machine?   It's really impressive, the whole production is just incredible! Very importantly, in order to pull off something as ridiculous as this film the performers needed to take this shit seriously and they really nailed it; those performances were cool as ice!

This film is so quotable, it's just badass in every way; instant favourite!
