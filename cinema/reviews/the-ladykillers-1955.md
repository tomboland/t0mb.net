This film is funny, charming and delightful, with farcical situations, characters and really great theatrical sets.  I enjoyed the direction too; a lot!  It reminded me of Fritz Lang's M, but in glorious 3-strip technicolour; a very different vibe, but there were a couple of nods to that film, I think, particulary the sinister introduction of Alec Guiness's character as a threatening top-hatted silouhette! The direction was just really pleasing all the way through though; it's a really beautiful film!  A very different comparison here, but the scene near the end with the last of the antagonists getting their comeuppance reminded me of the culminating scene in The Empire Strikes back!
