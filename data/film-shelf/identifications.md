## Owner confirmations — 19 October 2026

- **shelf-0173 — Les Misérables:** Owner confirms Lino Ventura adaptation; Robert Hossein (1982). Horizontal Blu-ray in photo 15. Corrects the earlier mistaken Claude Lelouch attribution.
- **shelf-0181 — Les Misérables:** Owner confirms Jean Gabin adaptation (1958), directed by Jean-Paul Le Chanois. Yellow Pathé box.
- **shelf-0245 — Un village français:** Owner confirms season 1; three-DVD set.
- **shelf-0256 — Breaking Bad:** Owner confirms all seasons apart from the separately packaged Final Season (shelf-0257). Retains home-video release wording without assuming broadcast-season numbering.
- **shelf-0393 — Point Break:** Owner confirms the 1991 Kathryn Bigelow film.
- **shelf-0604 — Lifeboat:** Owner confirms a second Lifeboat copy with a faulty Blu-ray disc. Clear horizontal case below shelf-0603; retained separately as an owned but faulty copy.
- **shelf-0642 — Cape Fear:** Owner confirms the 1991 Cape Fear adaptation. German package title: Kap der Angst.
