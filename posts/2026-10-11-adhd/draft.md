I recently sought a diagnosis of ADHD, seeking to be medicated for it. I'm writing about it because I hope to just explain a bit about how it's affected me and the people around me throughout life, and still does today. I'm not going to enter into the divisive discourse about it, but it's one reason I want to write about it.  I am framing this in the best way that I can understand it myself.

My overwhelming recollection of childhood is of living under a dark cloud.  I was constantly in trouble, at home and at school.  I was eccentric, disruptive and just too much sometimes.  When I was a young child I got into trouble a lot usually because I was being annoying, or weird, or both, then getting picked on because of it, and I'd lash out.  I could be violent towards others without reason as well, and have early memories of being yelled at without even understanding what I'd done after, one example after apparently having bitten another child's feet.  I don't know if I was 4 or 5 or something. My medical history has a lot of back and forth between my teachers, doctors and other medical professionals, all trying to figure out what could be done with me.  My medical record talks a lot of hyperactivity and hyperkinetic disorder.
