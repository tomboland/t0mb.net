I have a thing for digital sovereignty. I like films too. Letterboxd is the site where I log and review films. I like it because the interface is very usable, and it’s not a horribly obnoxious place where you have adverts and other things literally screaming for your attention all the time (Facebook: fuck you!).

I found out Netflix and others are in talks to buy Letterboxd, so I decided that my film log and reviews were no longer in a safe space. From now on, my own website is the source of truth. However, I’ll continue publishing reviews to Letterboxd for as long as the site continues to be good, not least because I enjoy reading other people’s reviews.
