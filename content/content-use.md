# Content use

You are welcome to read this site, link to it, find it through search engines,
and ask an assistant to read it to answer your questions. That includes using
the [film exports](/films/data/) for recommendations and exploring my collection.

**I do not consent to my original writing, photographs or other original content
being collected or used to train or fine-tune AI models, or included in datasets
for those purposes, without my express permission.** Permission to retrieve
content for a question is not permission to retain it for training.

Please fetch only what you need, reuse unchanged data and respect rate limits.
For the film exports, start with the manifest, keep files from the same revision,
and choose text or JSON rather than downloading both.

The site's [robots.txt](/robots.txt) expresses opt-outs through documented
training and dataset-collection controls while leaving search and
user-requested reading open. Google's control also covers Gemini grounding,
so that use is opted out alongside training; ordinary Google Search is unaffected.

These are requests to operators, not a technical barrier or a guarantee against
scraping. They cannot undo past collection.

This notice applies to content I own. Film metadata, artwork, quotations and
other third-party material remain subject to their own rights and terms.
It does not replace any explicit licence attached to a particular item.
